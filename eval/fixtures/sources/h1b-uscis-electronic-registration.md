# H-1B Electronic Registration Process

Alert Type info

**Alert:** USCIS has received enough petitions to reach the congressionally mandated 65,000 H-1B
visa regular cap and the 20,000 H-1B visa U.S. advanced degree exemption, known as the master's
cap, for fiscal year 2027.

If you are a prospective petitioner (also known as a registrant) seeking to file H-1B cap-subject
petitions, including for beneficiaries eligible for the advanced degree exemption, you must first
electronically register and pay the required H-1B registration fee for each prospective
beneficiary.

The initial registration period is for a minimum of 14 calendar days each fiscal year. USCIS then
runs the H-1B selection process to select unique beneficiaries based on properly submitted
electronic registrations.

#### How to Register

To submit an H-1B registration, you must first create a USCIS online account.

USCIS organizational accounts allow multiple people within an organization and their legal
representatives to collaborate on and prepare H-1B registrations and H-1B petitions. Registrants
need an organizational account to submit registrations for the H-1B cap selection process.

The initial registration period for the FY 2027 H-1B cap will open at noon Eastern on March 4 and
run through 5:00 p.m. Eastern on March 19.

#### Tips to Avoid Common Mistakes with H-1B Electronic Registration

Based on our experience with the H-1B electronic registration period, the top two user errors are:

- Creating the wrong type of account; and
- Entering the same beneficiary more than once.

A prospective petitioner may only have one registration submitted per beneficiary per fiscal year.
If a prospective petitioner has more than one registration submitted for the same beneficiary
after the initial registration period has closed, we will invalidate all registrations submitted
for that beneficiary from the selection process.
